# 🧠 FlowDesk — Product Overview

---

## 1. What is FlowDesk?

FlowDesk is a **Decision Intelligence System**.

It helps organizations:

- create decisions
- understand decisions
- track decisions
- evaluate decision outcomes

---

## 2. Problem

Organizations today:

- track metrics
- track initiatives

But they do NOT track:

```text
The decisions that caused them
```

---

This creates:

- lack of accountability
- no traceability
- poor governance
- repeated mistakes

---

## 3. Solution

FlowDesk introduces:

```text
Decision as a first-class entity
```

Every decision:

- has a lifecycle
- has a rationale
- is linked to outcomes
- is auditable

---

## 4. Core Capabilities

---

### Decision Lifecycle

- draft → proposed → approved → active → deprecated

---

### Explainability

- every decision must have a rationale

---

### Traceability

- decisions link to:
  - initiatives
  - metrics

---

### Governance

- approval workflows
- role-based control

---

### Observability

- decision impact tracking
- drift detection

---

## 5. Target Users

- product leaders
- engineering leaders
- governance teams
- data teams

---

Execution

Decision quality
