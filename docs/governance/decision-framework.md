# 🧠 FlowDesk — Decision Framework

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **framework used to structure, evaluate, and standardize decisions** in FlowDesk.

It answers:

- what makes a decision “valid”?
- what information must be present?
- how do we ensure decision quality?

---

## 2. Core Principle

A decision is not just a choice.

It is a **structured reasoning artifact**.

```text
Decision = Context + Options + Trade-offs + Rationale
```

---

## 3. Decision Components

Every decision in FlowDesk must include:

---

### 3.1 Context

- what problem are we solving?
- what is the current state?

---

### 3.2 Objective

- what outcome is expected?
- what metric defines success?

---

### 3.3 Options Considered

- alternative approaches
- rejected paths

---

### 3.4 Trade-offs

- pros / cons
- risks
- constraints

---

### 3.5 Final Decision

- chosen option
- scope

---

### 3.6 Rationale

- why this option was selected
- justification

---

### 3.7 Expected Impact

- linked metrics
- expected changes

---

## 4. Decision Quality Levels

---

### Level 1 — Basic

- minimal context
- weak rationale

---

### Level 2 — Structured

- context + rationale
- some trade-offs

---

### Level 3 — High Quality

- full structure
- clear impact
- linked metrics

---

### Level 4 — Governance-Ready

- fully structured
- measurable
- auditable
- traceable

---

## 5. Enforcement Model

---

### 5.1 At Creation

- required fields enforced
- incomplete decisions remain in draft

---

### 5.2 At Proposal

- rationale mandatory
- context validated

---

### 5.3 At Approval

- must include:
  - trade-offs
  - expected impact

---

## 6. Decision Template

```text
Title:
Context:
Objective:
Options:
Trade-offs:
Decision:
Rationale:
Expected Impact:
Linked Metrics:
```

---

## 7. Decision Evaluation

FlowDesk can evaluate decisions based on:

---

### Completeness

- all sections filled

---

### Clarity

- understandable reasoning

---

### Traceability

- linked to metrics / initiatives

---

### Consistency

- aligns with other decisions

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns Avoided

---

### ❌ Decisions without context

→ meaningless

---

### ❌ Decisions without trade-offs

→ naive reasoning

---

### ❌ Decisions without metrics

→ no validation

---

### ❌ Decisions as free text

→ not queryable
→ not enforceable

---

## 9. Staff-Level Insight

Most systems capture:

```text
Decision = Final Answer
```

FlowDesk captures:

```text
Decision = Reasoning Process
```

---

## 10. Summary

FlowDesk enforces:

```text
Structured Decisions → Better Governance → Better Outcomes
```
